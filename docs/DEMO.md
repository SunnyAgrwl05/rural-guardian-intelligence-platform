# Submission demo flow

1. **Overview** — introduce the multi-agent rural platform.
2. **Agent Hub** — show specialist workflows.
3. **AI Copilot** — show centralized assistant experience.
4. **Alerts & Safety** — explain risk-first handling and escalation.
5. **Knowledge & RAG** — show grounding architecture.
6. **Microsoft Ecosystem** — show integration-oriented service areas.
7. **Business Intelligence** — show the data-fusion concept and provenance-oriented views.
8. **Problem Center** — show guided diagnostics.
9. **Voucher Protection / Governance** — show the compliance and trust layer.
10. **Close** — explain that live Microsoft model/data connections can be enabled server-side when credentials and authoritative sources are configured.
